https://freesound.org/people/cognito%20perceptu/sounds/21187/

engine-suv.wav, engine-r8.wav: domasx2, racing car engine sound loops, loop_0.wav.
Mono, fixed-RPM resynthesis from an averaged combustion cycle; circular EQ and periodic resampling.
The source recording's pitch drift is removed; playback rate in the game controls RPM.
R8 is a pitched and filtered variant, not a recording of an Audi.
https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops

Originals: impactWood_heavy_000.ogg, impactMetal_heavy_000.ogg, impactPunch_heavy_000.ogg.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

click.mp3, purchase.mp3: Kenney, Interface Sounds.
Originals: click_001.ogg, confirmation_002.ogg.
https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

groan-1.mp3, groan-2.mp3: ianzazz, Zombie noises and moans.

radio.wav, wind.wav: original procedural noise made for this project; CC0 1.0.
Radio dialogue is subtitled; no speech recording is included.
Peak-normalized one-shots; engine loops have matched RMS. The truck retains its original seam crossfade.
Sound effects: mono 22050 Hz; engine/noise loops PCM WAV, one-shots MP3 64 kbit/s.
Conversion tool: FFmpeg 7.1 (temporary tool, not a game dependency).

https://opengameart.org/content/rock-theme-3
Original: rock_theme_3_loop_.wav; normalized, stereo 32000 Hz, MP3 96 kbit/s.
